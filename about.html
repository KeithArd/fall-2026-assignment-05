About Super Sports

Built by players, for players.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality sports equipment affordable and easy to get, so everyone can spend less time shopping and more time playing.

Our Story

Super Sports started as a small booth at local weekend tournaments. We noticed players were paying too much for basic gear, so we set out to offer the same quality for less. Today we stock everything from tennis rackets to hockey sticks and ship fast to players everywhere.
